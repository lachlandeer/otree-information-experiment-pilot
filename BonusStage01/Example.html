Your earnings

The closer your estimate is to the average token allocation of participants, the higher your payoff will be. Your payoff will be calculated according to the formula below:

Payoff = 1000 – 0.25 × (the sum of squared difference between the average allocated tokens and your estimate across all four pieces of information)

You can expect to earn more by assigning tokens in a way that matches how people who prefer the selected group culture usually do because the closer your estimate is to the average choice, the smaller your deduction will be.

For example, suppose the (unlikely) actual average tokens allocated in Task 1 are 0 tokens to 89, 0 tokens to 103, 0 tokens to 95, and 100 tokens to the mean value. Then your payoff will be calculated as:

Payoff = 500 - 0.25 × [(0 - 30)2 + (0 - 30)2 + (0 - 30)2 + (100 - 10)2]

Note: Since your Task 2 payoff depends on the Task 1 decisions of selected participants, you will not see the payoff after each task. It will be paid to you in the days following data collection.
