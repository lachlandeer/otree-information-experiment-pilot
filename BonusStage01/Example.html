Your earnings

The closer your estimate is to the average behavior of the participants, the higher your payoff from this decision problem will be. The payoff will be calculated according to the formula below:

Payoff = 1000 – 0.25*(the sum of squared difference between the average allocated tokens and your guess across all four pieces of information)

Suppose the actual average tokens allocated are 0 tokens to 89, 50 tokens to 103, 50 tokens to 95, and 0 tokens to the mean value. Then your payoff is:

Payoff = 1000 - 0.25 × [(0 - 30)2 + (50 - 30)2 + (50 - 30)2 + (0 - 10)2]
= 550 points

Please note that because the Task 2 payment depends on the decisions of other participants, you will not see the payoff after each task and it will be paid to you in the days after the data has been collected.
