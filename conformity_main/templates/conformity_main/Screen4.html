You do not directly choose which reference predictions to observe. Instead, you indicate your preferences, which influences the delivery of the reference.

There are 11 scenarios differed by the success rate of Computer Prediction, with 5% increment from 50% to 100%. You indicate for each scenario your preference over Peer Predictions and Computer Prediction by clicking the radio button “Peer” or “Computer”.

The computer will randomly select one scenario (each is equally likely). A prediction will then be delivered to you according to your choice in the selected scenario.

The moral is: if you make your choices based on what you truly prefer, you will never regret no matter which scenario is selected for delivering your reference.
