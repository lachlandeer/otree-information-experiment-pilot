In a similar study (“Study X”), 7 participants like you made predictions about the color of a ball drawn from the same virtual urn.

During Study X, these 7 participants formed a queue, and each made a prediction in turn after seeing the predictions made by those ahead in the queue.

For observing Peer Predictions, you will be like the 7th participant in Study X and see exactly what this participant saw before he/she made the color prediction. You will see 6 circles like the ones shown below.

In the real study, these circles will be filled with the colors predicted by the first 6 participants in Study X, which you may use as a reference for forming your own prediction.
