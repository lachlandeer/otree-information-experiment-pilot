Computer Prediction predicts the color of the drawn ball for you with a success rate ranging from 50% to 100%.

Suppose that the prediction success rate is 55% and the color of the drawn ball is blue. The following illustrates how Computer Prediction works.

In this example, with 55% chance, the actual color, blue, otherwise unknown to you, will be correctly predicted by the computer and seen by you.

With 45% chance, the prediction fails, and you will see the incorrect red instead. Note that during the study you will not be able to tell whether the predicted color is correct or not.
